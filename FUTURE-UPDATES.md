# folks — later update options

A running list of ideas for future versions. Not committed work — a menu to pull from
when planning the next update. Grouped by theme, with rough impact noted.

_Last updated: 19 Nov 2026_

---

## Top 3 to do next (highest leverage)

1. **Handover + return confirmation** — both people tap "handed over" / "returned" on a
   booking. Timestamps the exchange, cuts disputes, makes the deposit flow feel safe. _(medium effort, high trust value)_
2. **Save an item + "notify me"** — favourite a listing; get a push when something you
   searched for gets listed. Turns Wanted into a passive magnet and drives people back. _(quick win)_
3. **Share to WhatsApp** — a share button on every listing that drops a nice preview link
   into WhatsApp. How things actually spread in Arabian Ranches; doubles as free growth. _(quick win)_

---

## Trust & safety
*What makes people comfortable lending to a stranger two streets over.*

- **Resident verification** — a light, meaningful "verified neighbour" status: one-time
  manual approve or a community code, so members know everyone actually lives in AR.
- **Handover + return confirmation** — see Top 3. Both sides confirm the exchange in-app.
- **Report / block** — a quiet way to flag a dodgy listing or user. Table stakes once
  strangers can message each other.

## Liquidity & discovery
*An empty-feeling marketplace dies — give people things to find and reasons to list.*

- **Save / wishlist** — favourite items to come back to.
- **"Notify me"** — push when an item matching a saved search/Wanted post is listed.
- **Availability** — "free from / booked until" on each listing so people don't ask about
  something that's out.
- **Weekly digest** — "New this week in Arabian Ranches" email/push. Strongest single
  retention lever for a small community app.

## Lower friction (Dubai-specific)
- **Share to WhatsApp** — see Top 3.
- **Return reminders** — "due back tomorrow" to the borrower; "expect your drill back
  tomorrow" to the lender.

## Community feel
- **Free / giveaway section** — "take it, it's yours" for moving boxes, kids' clothes,
  plants. No deposit friction; great for word of mouth.
- **Live community stats** — "folks shared 142 things this month." Makes a small
  community feel alive.
